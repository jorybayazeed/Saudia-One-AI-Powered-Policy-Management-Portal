## Records

- Definition of KPI [REC-064; KPI Definition]
  A KPI is a metric or measure used to quantify and evaluate organizational and achievement success

- KPI Uses [REC-065; KPI Purpose]
  KPIs are used to set measurable objectives evaluate progress monitor trends make improvements and support decision making

- Management Alert [REC-066; KPI Requirement]
  A KPI measurement shall alert management if there are impending problems in a Key Performance Area
  Responsible role: Management | Trigger or condition: Impending problems in a Key Performance Area

a. Specific Measurement Data [REC-067; KPI Component]
  The indicator consists of specific measurement data
  Parent record: REC-066

b. Predetermined Standard [REC-068; KPI Component]
  The indicator consists of a predetermined standard against which the data will be compared
  Parent record: REC-066

- Average Response Time to Support Tickets [REC-069; KPI]
  Average response time to support tickets shall be within two working days of ticket creation
  Responsible role: IT Specialist | System or asset: Freshdesk Support Tickets | Frequency or timing: Measured from ticket creation | Target or threshold: Within 2 working days | Evidence or record: KPI Records / Support Ticket Timestamps

- IT Ticket Closure Rate [REC-070; KPI]
  Maintain a minimum closure rate of 97 percent for IT tickets per year
  Responsible role: IT Specialist | System or asset: IT Tickets | Frequency or timing: Per year | Target or threshold: Minimum 97 percent | Evidence or record: KPI Records

- Failure to Meet KPI Goals [REC-071; Corrective Action Requirement]
  Failure to meet established KPI goals will be addressed by starting problem solving and an appropriate improvement method
  Trigger or condition: When an established KPI goal is not met | Evidence or record: Problem Solving / Improvement Record
