## Records

1. Daily Routine Checks [REC-135; Routine Policy]
  Daily routine checks on the EDRAK system will be conducted by the IT Specialist
  Responsible role: IT Specialist | System or asset: EDRAK | Frequency or timing: Daily

2. Use of F02 Form [REC-136; Routine Policy]
  The EDRAK Routine Maintenance Card form number F02 is used to document daily routine activities
  Responsible role: IT Specialist | System or asset: EDRAK | Frequency or timing: Daily | Evidence or record: EDRAK Routine Maintenance Card F02

3. Form Soft Copy Location [REC-137; Routine Policy]
  A soft copy of the EDRAK Routine Maintenance Card is available in the stated folder
  System or asset: EDRAK | Evidence or record: EDRAK Routine Maintenance Card F02 | Storage location: PSAA Share File/Information Technology/Forms

4. Print Monthly Card [REC-138; Routine Policy]
  The IT Specialist prints a copy of the EDRAK Routine Maintenance Card at the beginning of each month
  Responsible role: IT Specialist | System or asset: EDRAK | Frequency or timing: Beginning of each month | Evidence or record: EDRAK Routine Maintenance Card F02

5. Complete Card Daily [REC-139; Routine Policy]
  The printed form is completed daily using the daily routine checklist
  Responsible role: IT Specialist | System or asset: EDRAK | Frequency or timing: Daily | Evidence or record: EDRAK Routine Maintenance Card F02

6. Scan Completed Card [REC-140; Routine Policy]
  At the end of each month the EDRAK Routine Maintenance Card is scanned to the stated records folder
  Responsible role: IT Specialist | System or asset: EDRAK | Frequency or timing: End of each month | Evidence or record: EDRAK Routine Maintenance Card F02 | Storage location: PSAA Share File/Information Technology/EDRAK Maintenance Routine Card Records

7. Open Ticket for Issues [REC-141; Routine Policy]
  The IT Specialist shall open a Freshdesk ticket for any issue found during a daily routine check
  Responsible role: IT Specialist | System or asset: Freshdesk | Trigger or condition: Issue found during daily routine check | Evidence or record: Support Ticket

8. Communication Room Access [REC-142; Access Requirement]
  Only Saudia Academy IT personnel shall have access to the Communication Room on the first floor
  Responsible role: SA IT Personnel | System or asset: Communication Room | Frequency or timing: Ongoing | Storage location: Communication Room First Floor
