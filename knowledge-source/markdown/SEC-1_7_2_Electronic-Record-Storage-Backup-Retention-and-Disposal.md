## Records

- IT Support Ticket Records [REC-042; Record Storage Requirement]
  All IT support ticket records are stored electronically in Saudia Academy's Freshdesk cloud-based account
  System or asset: Freshdesk | Evidence or record: IT Support Ticket Records | Storage location: Saudia Academy Freshdesk Account

- Meeting Minutes Records [REC-043; Record Storage Requirement]
  Meeting Minutes records are stored in PSAA Share File
  System or asset: PSAA Share File | Evidence or record: Meeting Minutes | Storage location: PSAA Share File

- CBT Routine Card Records [REC-044; Record Storage Requirement]
  CBT Routine Card records are stored in PSAA Share File
  System or asset: PSAA Share File | Evidence or record: CBT Routine Card Records | Storage location: PSAA Share File

- Automatic Backup Feature [REC-045; Backup Statement]
  Freshdesk and PSAA Share File servers have an inherent backup feature that provides an automatic backup record
  System or asset: Freshdesk / PSAA Share File | Frequency or timing: Automatic | Evidence or record: Automatic Backup Record

- Retention of IT Records [REC-046; Retention Requirement]
  All IT records are retained permanently
  System or asset: All IT Records | Retention period: Permanent

- Access and Retrieval of IT Records [REC-047; Access Requirement]
  All IT staff and management can access and retrieve all IT records at any time
  Responsible role: All IT Staff and Management | System or asset: All IT Records | Frequency or timing: Any time

- Disposal of IT Records [REC-048; Disposal Requirement]
  No IT records will be disposed
  System or asset: All IT Records | Retention period: No disposal

- EDRAK Routine Maintenance Card [REC-049; Record Keeping]
  The EDRAK Routine Maintenance Card is retained permanently
  Evidence or record: EDRAK Routine Maintenance Card | Storage location: PSAA Share File/Information Technology/Forms | Retention period: Permanent

- Meeting Minutes [REC-050; Record Keeping]
  Meeting Minutes are retained permanently
  Evidence or record: Meeting Minutes | Storage location: PSAA Share File/Information Technology/IT Records/IT Meetings Records | Retention period: Permanent

- KPI Records [REC-051; Record Keeping]
  KPI records are retained permanently
  System or asset: Freshdesk | Evidence or record: KPI Records | Storage location: Saudia Academy Account in Freshdesk | Retention period: Permanent
