## Records

1. Receive Ticket [REC-111; Process Step]
  The IT Specialist receives tickets in the Freshdesk system
  Responsible role: IT Specialist | System or asset: Freshdesk | Evidence or record: Support Ticket

2. Define Asset or Process Ownership [REC-112; Process Step]
  The IT Specialist determines whether the asset or process is owned by SVIT or Saudia Academy
  Responsible role: IT Specialist | System or asset: Freshdesk | Evidence or record: Support Ticket

2A. SVIT-Owned Asset or Process [REC-113; Process Step]
  If the asset or process is owned by SVIT advise the user to contact SVIT and proceed to step 7
  Responsible role: IT Specialist | System or asset: Freshdesk | Trigger or condition: Asset or process is owned by SVIT | Evidence or record: Support Ticket | Reference or recipient: SVIT | Parent record: REC-112

2B. Academy IT-Owned Asset or Process [REC-114; Process Step]
  If the asset or process is owned by Academy IT proceed to the next step
  Responsible role: IT Specialist | System or asset: Freshdesk | Trigger or condition: Asset or process is owned by Academy IT | Evidence or record: Support Ticket | Parent record: REC-112

3. Identify Cause [REC-115; Process Step]
  The IT Specialist identifies the cause of the problem
  Responsible role: IT Specialist | System or asset: Freshdesk | Evidence or record: Support Ticket

4. Determine Solution [REC-116; Process Step]
  The IT Specialist determines the solution
  Responsible role: IT Specialist | System or asset: Freshdesk | Evidence or record: Support Ticket

5. Apply Solution [REC-117; Process Step]
  The IT Specialist applies the solution
  Responsible role: IT Specialist | System or asset: Freshdesk | Evidence or record: Support Ticket

6. Confirm Fix [REC-118; Process Step]
  The IT Specialist confirms whether the problem is fixed
  Responsible role: IT Specialist | System or asset: Freshdesk | Evidence or record: Support Ticket

6A. Problem Not Fixed [REC-119; Process Step]
  If the problem is not fixed return to step 3
  Responsible role: IT Specialist | System or asset: Freshdesk | Trigger or condition: Problem is not fixed | Evidence or record: Support Ticket | Parent record: REC-118

6B. Problem Fixed [REC-120; Process Step]
  If the problem is fixed proceed to the next step
  Responsible role: IT Specialist | System or asset: Freshdesk | Trigger or condition: Problem is fixed | Evidence or record: Support Ticket | Parent record: REC-118

7. Update and Close Ticket [REC-121; Process Step]
  The IT Specialist updates and closes the ticket
  Responsible role: IT Specialist | System or asset: Freshdesk | Evidence or record: Support Ticket
